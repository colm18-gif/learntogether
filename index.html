How these pathways help

Every unit is built the same way, so once you know how one works, you know them all.

  1. Clear structureEach subject is broken into short units that match classroom learning without requiring a textbook on the table.
  2. Plain explanationsKey ideas are defined in everyday language, with worked examples and "watch out" notes for common mistakes.
  3. Active practiceEvery unit includes a short task to do together and a quick quiz to check understanding.
  4. Portfolio readySuggestions for evidence to save — drafts, corrections, research, presentations, reflections — support the Classroom‑Based Assessments.
  5. Flexible useJump to the unit that matches this week's topic, or move sequentially as a revision pathway.

About Learn Together

Learn Together is an independent, non-commercial project built to give parents and guardians a plain-language way to support Junior Cycle study at home — it isn't a school, exam board or official curriculum publisher.

Each pathway is written to follow the strands and learning outcomes of the relevant NCCA Junior Cycle specification, but the guides are a companion to classroom teaching, not a replacement for it — your teenager's teacher remains the best guide to the exact sequence, depth and methods expected in their school and for their exam year.

The site is a work in progress: new subjects and units are still being added, and if you spot something that's unclear, incorrect or out of date, the quickest way to flag it is the contact form below.

These resources are designed to complement, not replace, classroom teaching. Always check with the teacher for the exact level, sequence and methods expected in your school.
